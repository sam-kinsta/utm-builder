UTM URL Builder

Build correctly formatted campaign tracking URLs for Google Analytics 4.

Before you start: Never add UTM parameters to internal links (links between kinsta.com pages). UTMs are for external links only - inside emails, ads, social posts, and partner sites.

The domain of the site or platform sending the traffic (e.g. facebook.com) Campaign source is required
