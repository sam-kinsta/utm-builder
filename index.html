UTM URL Builder

Build correctly formatted campaign tracking URLs for Google Analytics 4.

Before you start: Never add UTM parameters to internal links (links between kinsta.com pages). UTMs are for external links only - inside emails, ads, social posts, and partner sites. This tool automatically lowercases all values and replaces spaces with underscores to keep your GA4 data clean.
